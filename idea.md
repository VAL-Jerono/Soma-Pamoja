## **Theme:** Social Impact

## **Problem Statement:** Community Support Platform

## **Project Title:** Soma Pamoja: Turning Every Community into a Place to Learn to Read


A child displaced by conflict should not lose the chance to learn to read. Yet across East and Central Africa, conflict, disease outbreaks and climate disasters interrupt schooling, leaving children without teachers, learning materials or the foundational literacy they need to shape their futures.

**Soma Pamoja (“Read Together”) turns community spaces into opportunities for learning, even when schools cannot function.** It is an AI-powered, multilingual reading tutor that helps children learn through voice, pictures and text, guided by a local facilitator. Children progress from letters and sounds to words, sentences and comprehension, building the foundations for lifelong learning.

Designed for cross-border adoption, Soma Pamoja adapts to the languages of refugee settlements and remote communities across Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo. Facilitators can access simplified support through USSD and SMS, while printable activities extend learning to communities with limited digital access.

**Trust is built into the learning model.** A curated Reading Bank provides verified literacy activities aligned with recognised foundational education standards. The AI retrieves approved content rather than inventing lessons. Native speakers validate translations, facilitators confirm reading progress, and pseudonymous learner codes help protect children's identities. Voice recordings are not stored.

We will begin with refugee and displaced communities, then expand to under-resourced schools and community learning spaces. Success will be measured through improvements in reading fluency and comprehension, learner participation, and cost per child reached.

**Our ambition is bigger than a reading app. It is a regional community learning network that ensures interrupted schooling does not become interrupted potential.** Every child deserves the chance to read, learn and build a future, regardless of where they live or whether a school is open.
